ABOUT ZEDHEALTH

Healthcare with purpose

ZedHealth is committed to advancing accessible, reliable and innovative healthcare solutions that create meaningful impact across Zambia and beyond.

OUR STORY

Built to strengthen
the system, not
sit beside it.

ZedHealth recognizes that conventional health insurance remains an important healthcare financing option, but it may not always be accessible or suitable for every individual, family or organization. Some people and smaller organizations may face challenges obtaining conventional cover because of pre-existing conditions, existing illness, group size, affordability, eligibility or underwriting requirements, or limited options available to individuals and small enterprises.

ZedHealth aims to help bridge this gap through flexible healthcare administration and managed healthcare solutions structured around the needs and budgets of different groups.

Our ambition is to broaden access to organized healthcare beyond traditional large corporate schemes and provide practical healthcare options for the wider population, including individuals, families, SMEs, associations and organized groups.

OUR MISSION

To transform the management of healthcare through technology, transparency and shortening the journey to appropriate treatment and improving quality outcomes for organizations, and individuals.

OUR VISION

To build an inclusive healthcare ecosystem where quality and affordable healthcare is accessible to corporates, and individuals through efficient administration, technology and responsible managed care.
