WHO WE ARE

Healthcare designed
around people,
purpose and progress.

ZedHealth Administration Services is a healthcare administration and managed-care company dedicated to providing affordable, flexible, efficient and technology-driven healthcare solutions to corporate organizations, groups and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). Through a growing network of healthcare providers in Zambia, India and South Africa, ZedHealth facilitates access to local and international medical services according to the requirements of each healthcare arrangement.

Accessibility

Expanding access to organized healthcare solutions for corporates, and individuals.

Affordability

Structuring healthcare solutions to deliver meaningful benefits at sustainable costs.

Flexibility

Configuring solutions around different healthcare needs, budgets and member groups.

Quality Care

Developing strong local and international provider relationships to facilitate appropriate treatment.
